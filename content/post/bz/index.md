Our FIFA World Cup 2026 prediction work received press coverage in **BZ Berlin** and other outlets ahead of the tournament's kick-off on June 11.

👉 **[Read the BZ Berlin article here](https://www.bz-berlin.de/berlin/berliner-forscher-wissen-schon-wer-die-wm-gewinnt)**

The team at the **Freie Universität Berlin** combined three statistical models and evaluated approximately **13,000 international matches since 2012** to generate win probabilities for all 48 competing nations. The approach integrates the **Dixon–Coles method** and **Elo ratings** to estimate team strength, which then feeds into a Monte Carlo simulation that replays the entire tournament 100,000 times.

Key model outputs for the 2026 World Cup:

| Team | Win Probability |
|------|----------------|
| 🇦🇷 Argentina | ~14% |
| 🇪🇸 Spain | ~13% |
| 🏴󠁧󠁢󠁥󠁮󠁧󠁿 England | ~12% |
| 🇩🇪 Germany | ~5.5% |

Germany is projected to reach the Round of 32 with ~98% probability and is expected to score around 11 goals over the course of the tournament.
